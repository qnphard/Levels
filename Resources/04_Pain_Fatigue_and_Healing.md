# Chronic Pain, Fatigue, and Healing

Causes discussed:
- Emotional suppression (held energy)
- Resistance to love or truth
- Over-identification with effort/force
- Spiritual pride or misalignment
- Physical overload without integration

Healing principles:
- Physical care (sleep, nutrition, load management)
- Emotional processing (allowing without narrative)
- Spiritual alignment (surrender, supplication, humility)
