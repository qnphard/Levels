### Path Through Design (8 Steps)

1. **Cultivate Beginner's Mind**: Approach familiar situations with fresh eyes and curiosity. Ask yourself: "What can I learn here that I haven't noticed before?" Practice saying "I don't know" with comfort and interest rather than shame.

2. **Embrace Feedback as a Gift**: Actively seek out constructive feedback from trusted sources. Listen without defending or explaining. Thank people for their honesty and consider how their perspectives might help you grow, even if you don't initially agree.

3. **Practice Saying Yes to Growth Opportunities**: When opportunities for learning or development arise, notice your first impulse. If it's fear or resistance, pause and ask: "What would happen if I said yes to this?" Start with small risks and build your willingness muscle.

4. **Release Attachment to Being Right**: Notice when you're invested in proving your point or defending your position. Practice curiosity instead: "Help me understand your perspective" or "What am I missing here?" Value learning over being right.

5. **Experiment with New Approaches**: When facing challenges, resist the urge to use the same old methods. Ask: "What's a different way I could approach this?" Try new strategies, even if they feel uncomfortable or unfamiliar at first.

6. **Develop Comfort with Uncertainty**: Practice sitting with not knowing the outcome or having all the answers. Find peace in the process of discovery rather than needing to control or predict results. Trust that clarity will emerge through action and experience.

7. **Seek Out Diverse Perspectives**: Intentionally expose yourself to different viewpoints, cultures, and ways of thinking. Read books outside your usual interests, talk to people who think differently, travel to new places. Let diversity expand your understanding.

8. **Commit to Lifelong Learning**: Adopt the identity of a lifelong learner. Set aside time regularly for growth activities—reading, courses, workshops, or conversations with mentors. See every day as an opportunity to become a better version of yourself.
